Our story

4 Years. 200+ Happy Customers. One Mission.

Mano Madhu Tours and Travels was founded with a simple idea: people in Coimbatore deserve a taxi and travel service they can trust — clean vehicles, honest rates and drivers who care.

Starting with a single vehicle on NSR Road, Saibaba Colony, we've grown into a full-service travel company operating across Coimbatore and South India, handling everything from airport drops to multi-day pilgrimage tours.

We are proud of our 5-star Google rating — earned through hundreds of trips where we showed up on time, charged what we quoted, and got our customers home safely.
